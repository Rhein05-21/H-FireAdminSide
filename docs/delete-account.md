# H-Fire Admin — Account & Data Deletion Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**App Name:** H-Fire Admin / H-Fire Community Safety  
**Support Email:** hfire.iotsystem@gmail.com

---

## 1. Overview
In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, users of the H-Fire Admin application have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

---

## 2. How to Request Account Deletion

### Method 1: Email Request (Web / App Uninstalled)
If you wish to delete your account or have uninstalled the application:
1. Send an email to: **hfire.iotsystem@gmail.com**
2. Use the subject line: **`H-Fire Account Deletion Request`**
3. In the email body, provide:
   * Your **Registered Email Address**
   * Your **Full Name**
   * Your **Household / Subdivision Block & Lot** (if applicable)
4. Our administrative team will verify your account and process the permanent deletion within **30 days**. You will receive a confirmation email once complete.

---

### Method 2: In-App Deletion
If you have the app installed:
1. Open **H-Fire Admin** and log in.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
3. Tap **Delete Account & Associated Data** and confirm.

---

## 3. What Personal Data is Deleted
Upon deletion, the following data is permanently purged:
* User profile records (name, email, phone number, address)
* Authentication credentials and session tokens
* Mobile device push notification tokens
* Hardware IoT sensor linkages

---

## 4. Retained Data Disclosure
* Historical emergency fire and gas incident alarm logs are retained in an anonymized format strictly for community disaster risk reporting, investigation, and safety compliance.
